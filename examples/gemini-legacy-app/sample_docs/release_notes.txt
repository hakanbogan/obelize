Release 4.2.0 -- 14 March 2025

The scheduler no longer holds the job table lock while it writes the audit row,
which was the cause of the nightly stalls reported by three customers. Jobs that
were stuck in the "starting" state are recovered on boot instead of being left
for an operator.

Two smaller fixes: the CSV export now quotes fields containing a semicolon, and
the health endpoint reports the database connection pool size rather than a
hard-coded value.

Known issue: on PostgreSQL 13 the new recovery pass can take up to 40 seconds on
a table with more than two million rows. A fix is planned for 4.2.1.
